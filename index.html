01 · THE LAB

A practical home for ideas that become software.

JASS Digital Lab brings together small utilities, serious research tools, language datasets, desktop applications, document workflows, creative experiments and long-lived software projects.

The emphasis is on useful software: understandable, maintainable, privacy-conscious and capable of working locally wherever practical.

04 · PROJECT ATHENA
A LOCAL-FIRST SECOND BRAIN

Knowledge should remain useful, searchable and understandable.

Athena is the long-running knowledge-management and research direction inside JASS Digital Lab. Its architecture emphasizes local-first workflows, retrieval, citations, document intelligence, workspace intelligence and integration with local AI tools.

05 · DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY
01
